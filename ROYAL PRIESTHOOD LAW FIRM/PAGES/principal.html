PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY

From Public Service to Legal Practice.

The Principal of Royal Priesthood Law Firm retired from the Lagos State Government after a distinguished career of over three decades in the public service.

His career culminated in his service as Director of Human Resources in the State Ministry. During his years in public service, he gained extensive experience in administration, human resource management, governance, public policy and institutional leadership.

This background provides a valuable perspective on the complexities of government, corporate administration, employment and human resource matters.

Following his retirement, he made the decision to embark on a new professional journey in law.

61

A COMMITMENT TO LEARNING

Beginning Again. Pursuing Excellence.

At the age of 61, he commenced his legal education, demonstrating that excellence has no age limit and that the pursuit of knowledge and service can begin anew at any stage of life.

Following the successful completion of his legal studies and professional training, he was called to the Nigerian Bar, marking the beginning of his legal practice.
