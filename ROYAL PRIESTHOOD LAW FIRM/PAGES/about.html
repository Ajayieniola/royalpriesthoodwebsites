WHO WE ARE

Professional Legal Service Built on Experience.

Royal Priesthood Law Firm is a full-service law firm committed to providing professional, ethical and client-focused legal services.

The Firm is founded and led by a seasoned professional whose distinguished career spans over 35 years of public service, bringing to legal practice a wealth of experience in administration, human resource management, governance, public policy and institutional leadership.

The Principal of the Firm retired from the Ogun State Government after a distinguished career of over three decades in the public service, culminating in his service as Director of Human Resources in the State Ministry.

His extensive experience in public administration and institutional management provides the Firm with a valuable understanding of the complexities of government, corporate administration, employment and human resource matters.

A NEW PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY

Excellence Has No Age Limit.

Driven by a lifelong commitment to learning, service and professional excellence, the Principal embarked on a new professional journey in law after his retirement.

At the age of 61, he commenced his legal education, demonstrating that excellence has no age limit and that the pursuit of knowledge and service can begin anew at any stage of life.

Following the successful completion of his legal studies and professional training, he was called to the Nigerian Bar, marking the beginning of his legal practice.
